Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

RepLock (the "App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2-1. Camera Data

  • The App uses your device's camera to detect exercise poses and count repetitions in real time
  • Camera data is processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework
  • No photos or videos are captured, stored, or transmitted
  • Camera access requires your explicit permission

2-2. Workout Data

  • Exercise type, rep count, workout duration, and completion status
  • Workout history and streak information
  • This data is stored locally on your device only

2-3. Preferences and Settings

  • Exercise preferences, lock schedule, and selected apps to block
  • Onboarding profile information (name, goals, frequency)
  • This data is stored locally on your device only

2-4. Screen Time Data

  • The App uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls framework) to manage app restrictions
  • App selection tokens are stored locally and are opaque identifiers — the App cannot see which specific apps you have selected

2-5. Analytics and Diagnostics

  • The App may collect anonymous usage data (e.g., screen views, feature usage) to improve the service
  • Crash reports may be collected to identify and fix bugs
  • This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally

3. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing core functionality (workout tracking, app locking, rep counting)
  • Improving app quality and fixing bugs
  • Processing subscription purchases through Apple

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • Service providers: Apple processes subscription payments through the App Store
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or legal process

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Apple StoreKit: Subscription and in-app purchase processing
  • Apple Screen Time API: App restriction management
  • Apple Vision Framework: On-device pose detection

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All user data is stored locally on your device
  • No personal data is transmitted to external servers
  • Workout data is stored using SwiftData (on-device database)
  • Preferences are stored using UserDefaults (on-device storage)

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the App from your device. Since all data is stored locally, no server-side data deletion is required.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If significant changes are made, you will be notified through the App or on this page.
